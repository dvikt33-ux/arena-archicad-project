## Traps

- **СП 306.1325800.2017** is active, but it is a **multifunctional shopping-complex operation** standard. It must not substitute for the current design SP for a ТРК.
- **СП 257.1325800.2016** is replaced; use СП 257.1325800.2020.
- A specialized SP does not cancel fire, accessibility, sanitary, structural, thermal, acoustic, daylight or local-planning requirements. The rule engine must merge all applicable layers and resolve conflicts/applicability at clause level.
